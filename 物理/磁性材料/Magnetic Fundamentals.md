## Definition of magnetic feilds and magnetic moment

- $H$ : magnetic field, $\mathrm{A/m}$ ;
	- an energy gradient;
	- how much torque on a magnetic dipole inside the field;

- $B$ : magnetic flux density, $\rm Tesla$ ($\rm T$) or $\rm Weber/m^2$ ($\rm Wb/m$) ;
	- the number of magnetic field lines per unit area;

- $M$ : magnetization or magnetic moment density, $\rm A/m$ ;
	- response of a materials to a field


## Origin of magnetic field

1. Coming from a current

- We have a current $I$ here down this wire, 
- then there's magnetic field $H$ that circulates around it.
$$H=\frac{I}{2\pi R}\quad \rm (A/m)$$


2. Coming from magnetized material (magnet)

- magnetized material produces a magnetic field which is quite non-uniform;
- the field comes out from one end (north pole) and goes into another (south pole).
