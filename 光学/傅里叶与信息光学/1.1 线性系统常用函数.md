$$
\operatorname{rect} \left(\frac {x - x _ {0}}{a}\right) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} 1, & \left| \frac {x - x _ {0}}{a} \right| \leqslant \frac {1}{2} \\ 0, & \text {其他} \end{array} \right.\tag{1.1.1}
$$

$$

# 1.1.2 $\delta$ 函数

## $\delta$ 函数定义

```ad-def
$\delta$ 函数:

$$\delta(x,y)=\left\{\begin{array}{ll}0,&x\neq0\text{ 或 }y\neq0\\\infty,&x=y=0\end{array}\right.$$

且
$$
 {\iint_ {- \infty} ^ {\infty} \delta (x, y) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y = 1} \tag{1.2.1}
$$
```


## $\delta$ 函数性质

1. **筛选**
$$\iint_ {- \infty} ^ {\infty} f (x, y) \delta (x - x _ {0}, y - y _ {0}) \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y = f (x _ {0}, y _ {0})\tag{1.2.8}$$
2. **坐标缩放**
$$
\delta (a x, b y) = \frac {1}{| a b |} \delta (x, y)\tag{1.2.9}
$$

3. **分离变量**
$$
\delta (x, y) = \delta (x) \delta (y)\tag{1.2.10}
$$
4. 与普通函数乘积的性质, 函数 $f(x,y)$ 在 $(x_{0},y_{0})$ 点连续，则有
$$
f (x, y) \delta (x - x _ {0}, y - y _ {0}) = f (x _ {0}, y _ {0}) \delta (x - x _ {0}, y - y _ {0})\tag{1.2.11}
$$


# 1.1.3 梳状函数

一维梳妆函数:
$$
\operatorname{comb} (x) = \sum_ {n = - \infty} ^ {\infty} \delta (x - n)\tag{1.2.12}
$$

二维梳妆函数 (直角坐标):
$$
\operatorname{comb} (x, y) = \operatorname{comb} (x) \operatorname{comb} (y)\tag{1.2.13}
$$
